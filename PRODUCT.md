# Business Buddy — Your AI Business Consultant, Always On

> *The AI that knows your business as well as you do.*

---

## The Problem

Every business owner needs expert advice — on finances, marketing, operations, hiring,
and strategy. But great consultants are expensive, unavailable at 2am, and know nothing
about your specific situation. Generic AI tools give generic answers. You spend more
time explaining context than getting value.

---

## What Business Buddy Does

Business Buddy is a dedicated AI consultant that **learns everything about your business**
and never forgets it. Every conversation, every uploaded document, every connected data
source makes it smarter about *your* company — not companies in general.

Ask it anything. It answers with your actual numbers, your real customers, your specific
challenges — not templates and best-practice boilerplate.

## Key Benefits

### Always in context
Upload your pitch deck, financials, org chart, or product roadmap once. Business Buddy
reads everything and references it in every future answer — automatically.

### Remembers everything
Unlike ChatGPT, Business Buddy doesn't forget between sessions. Your knowledge base
grows with every conversation. Come back a month later — it still knows your ARR,
your team size, and your biggest open challenge.

### Role-aware advice
Ask about cash flow and it thinks like a CFO. Ask about your next hire and it thinks
like an HR director. Ask about market positioning and it thinks like a CMO. One tool,
every hat.

### Private and team-ready
Invite your co-founders, operators, and advisors. Everyone works from the same shared
knowledge base. One source of truth, always up to date.

### Works with what you already have
Connect your Gmail or Slack and Business Buddy automatically ingests the context it
needs — no manual data entry, no copy-pasting.

## How It Works

```
1. Sign up → describe your business in a quick onboarding conversation
2. Upload → drop in documents (PDFs, decks, spreadsheets, reports)
3. Connect → link Gmail or Slack for ongoing context
4. Ask → get specific, grounded advice any time of day
```

The more you use it, the smarter it gets about your business.

## Built on a Foundation You Can Trust

- **Privacy first** — your data is isolated per business, never shared or used to train models
- **Open source stack** — Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React — no vendor lock-in
- **Self-hostable** — run it on your own infrastructure with Docker Compose in minutes
